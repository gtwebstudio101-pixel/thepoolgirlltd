What We Offer

Professional Pool Services

From regular maintenance to full equipment replacement, we keep your pool in pristine condition all year round.

01

Maintenance

We offer regular and one off maintenance and cleaning of your swimming pool.

Not only does crystal clear blue water look and feel better, regular maintenance is essential for not only the health of swimmers but for your plant equipment too.

Well maintained swimming pools have fewer problems therefore tend to need fewer repairs. Our prices are extremely competitive and start from only £65.

From £65
02

Opening & Closing

We recommend closing your outdoor swimming pool during the winter months when it's not being used in order to protect your equipment and pipe work from the freezing temperatures.

Closing your pool for winter means no maintenance saving you money until spring comes, we can then come and transform your pool into crystal clear blue water to be enjoyed for another summer.

If you do decide to keep your outdoor pool open all year round, please see our maintenance section above.

03

Pool Equipment Replacement

We can source and replace many parts of your pool equipment this includes but isn't limited to:

  • Swimming pool geobubble covers
  • Pipework and valves
  • Sand filters
  • Pumps
04

Hot Tub Maintenance

The Pool Girl is able to carry out general maintenance of your hot tub. This includes water balancing, cleaning of filters, vacuuming etc. We can also carry out a "hot tub flush" to ensure your hot tub is clean from the inside out.

Please enquire if you would like to know more information.

My Story

Passion Turned into Profession

It all started in 2021 when I discovered my father was being drastically overcharged for poor pool maintenance. I took matters into my own hands — learned the trade, gained industry qualifications, and founded The Pool Girl Ltd to offer honest, expert service.

Today, I am CIMSPA-endorsed, PWTAG-recognised as an on-site designated supervisor, and trained alongside Malcolm North of Pool Pros — a specialist with over 35 years of experience. Health, hygiene and value for money are at the heart of everything I do.
